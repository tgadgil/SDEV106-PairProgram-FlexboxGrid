Upma

Simple, quick, and delicious semolina breakfast.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup rava (semolina)
  • 2 tbsp oil or ghee
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 1 green chili, chopped
  • Salt to taste
  • 2 cups water

Instructions

  1. Roast the rava lightly until fragrant.
  2. Heat oil, sauté onion and chili.
  3. Add water and salt; bring to a boil.
  4. Stir in rava slowly, cook until fluffy.
